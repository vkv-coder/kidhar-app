Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What We Collect
  • Staff/admin account details — name, email (used to sign in and identify who is checking in)
  • Precise GPS location — only at the moment you tap "Share My Location Now," together with the date and time. Kidhar never tracks you continuously or in the background.
  • A photo — only if your company's admin has turned on "Require a photo on check-in." If so, a photo is taken at the same moment as each check-in and stored alongside it.

We do not collect contacts or any other device data.

Where Your Data Lives

All data is stored on Supabase's hosted infrastructure, protected by row-level security rules so your company's data can never be seen by another company using Kidhar.

Who Can Access Your Data

Your check-in locations are visible only to you and to your own company's approved admins — never to the public, to other companies on Kidhar, or to any third party. Account passwords are stored securely (hashed) — we cannot read them.

Data Deletion

To request correction or deletion of your account or check-in history, contact your company's admin, or write to info.anyapps@gmail.com — requests are handled within 7 working days.
